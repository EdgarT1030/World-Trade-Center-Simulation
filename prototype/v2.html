Computed. Roof sway from the plane's momentum. Fire temperature at the wall on each of seven floors. Steel temperature of each floor truss and outer column, every 5 seconds. Set by you, not predicted. How many floors lost their insulation, and how long the fire takes to reach the wall that fails. NIST says the time to collapse was governed by how long the fires took to reach and weaken that wall. Estimates inside the model. Fire reaches each floor above and below 5 minutes after its neighbour. A spot burns at peak for 5 minutes per lb/sq ft of contents and burns out after about 22 minutes per lb/sq ft. Outer columns are heated on their one inside face and lose heat from the other three, so they run much cooler than the trusses. Column plates are assumed thicker lower in the tower.

    The building and the day

    What was standing there

    • Size. 110 storeys, 1,368 ft (WTC 1) and 1,362 ft (WTC 2), about 207 ft square, roughly 12 ft per storey. About 40,000 sq ft per floor. [1][8]
    • Outer wall. 236 steel box columns, 59 per face, each about 14 in square and 40 in apart, tied together at every floor by 52-in-deep steel plates. This wall resisted the wind and carried about half the weight. [1]
    • Core. 47 heavier columns in a 135 ft by 87 ft rectangle around the elevators and stairs, carrying the other half. [1]
    • Floors. 4 in of lightweight concrete on steel trusses spanning 60 ft or 35 ft from core to outer wall, with no columns in between. The floors also held the outer columns in line. [1]
    • Fire protection. Sprayed insulation on the steel: about ¾ in on the floor trusses in WTC 2's fire floors, about 2½ in on the upgraded WTC 1 fire floors. [2]
    • The day. Clear, wind from the north at roughly 11 to 22 mph at tower height. About 8,900 people in WTC 1 and 8,500 in WTC 2. [1][6][8]
    • Timeline. WTC 1 hit 8:46 a.m., fell 10:28 a.m. (102 minutes). WTC 2 hit 9:02 a.m., fell 9:58 a.m. (56 minutes). [2]

    Did the wind that morning matter?

    Show the math

    NIST calculated that the original design wind loads would move the top 51 to 65 in. [6] NIST left wind out of its collapse analysis after checking airport wind records for the day. [5]

    Check 4 of 7

    Was there enough fuel to make a serious fire?

    Heat available inside the tower, gigajoules
    Show the math

    NIST reports that less than 15% of the jet fuel burned in the spray cloud inside the building and a comparable amount in the fireballs outside. [1] It also says hydrocarbon and ordinary building fires both top out near 1,100 °C. [2]

    Read before trusting any of this

    What this sheet cannot tell you

    • It is seven hand calculations, not a simulation. Real fires move, real floors pull unevenly, and loads shift between columns in ways one formula cannot follow.
    • Several inputs are my estimates. The tower's weight per storey, the column plate thickness at the impact floors, the insulation's conductivity and how long one spot stayed at peak temperature are not taken from a source.
    • Flight 175's weight is assumed equal to Flight 11's. Its fuel load is reported between about 8,200 and 10,000 gallons depending on the source. [8]
    • The severed-column counts come from NIST's impact simulations, not from inspection. NIST says most physical evidence was destroyed or removed before its investigation began. [2]
    • The recovered steel does not confirm the temperatures. NIST found only three of more than 170 examined outer-panel locations had exceeded 250 °C, and no sample showed long exposure above 600 °C. NIST says the samples were 3% of the outer columns on the fire floors. [13]
    • It covers the Twin Towers only. Building 7, which was not hit by a plane, needs its own analysis.
    Warnings record

    What the government knew, and when

    Two separate questions get mixed together. Did the government know an attack was coming? And did anyone know planes were heading for the towers that morning in time to stop them? The public record answers them differently.

    • Before the day: the record shows years of warnings that al Qaeda wanted to attack inside the United States, several that mentioned hijackings or aircraft as weapons, and specific leads on future hijackers that were not acted on.
    • On the day: air defense controllers had nine minutes' notice of the first plane and none of the other three, according to the 9/11 Commission's reconstruction from tapes and logs. [10]
    • What is missing: no document made public so far shows anyone in the government knowing the date, the targets or the method in advance.
    Documented In an official record or a declassified document. Disputed Real events, contested meaning. Not supported Investigated and not borne out.

    Claims that go further

    Where the evidence gets thinner

    ClaimWhat is on recordStatus
    Someone bet on airline stocks fallingTrading in put options on United and American did spike on September 6 and 10. The Commission reports that "a single U.S.-based institutional investor with no conceivable ties to al Qaeda purchased 95 percent of the UAL puts on September 6." Its conclusion: "the apparently suspicious consistently proved innocuous." [15]Not supported
    A military data-mining unit had identified Mohamed Atta in 2000Lt. Col. Anthony Shaffer and Rep. Curt Weldon said the Able Danger programme had identified four hijackers. In September 2006 the Pentagon inspector general reported "no evidence" that it had identified Atta or any other hijacker. [16]Disputed
    Five Israeli men in New Jersey knew in advanceFive Israeli citizens were detained on September 11 after a witness saw men filming the burning towers. They were held and later deported. No court ruling or official finding established that they were intelligence officers or knew of the attack beforehand. [17]Disputed
    Fighters were held back on purposeThe tapes and logs show the military was told too late to intercept any of the four planes. [10]Not supported

    The strongest documented case is that the government had repeated warnings and specific leads, and failed to connect them. The claim that officials knew the date and targets needs a document or witness the public record does not yet contain.
